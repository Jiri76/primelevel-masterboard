Drawn to scale (1 real px = 0.7 px here). The striped bands are the empty spaces: orange = 100px, teal = 30px, gold = the normal 24px line spacing. Every gap is measured the way the eye sees it — from the bottom of the letters above to the top of the letters below (or to the edge of the card or underline) — and checked live on the page at desktop width. These are fixed page settings, so every report gets exactly the same spacing whatever its length.
Font sizes (locked): chapter headings 25px · tickers 20px · text and the three closing lines 15px · page title "Insider Edge" 60px · title in the dark band 35px · date line 15px.
